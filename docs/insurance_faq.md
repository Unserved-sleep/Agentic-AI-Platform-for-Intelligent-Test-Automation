# Insurance FAQ

## General
**Q: How do I renew my policy?**
A: You can renew your policy online through our portal 30 days before it expires.

**Q: Can I cancel my policy mid-term?**
A: Yes, cancellations are allowed subject to a pro-rata premium refund based on the terms and conditions.

## Health Insurance
**Q: Is maternity covered?**
A: Maternity coverage depends on the specific health plan chosen. Please refer to your policy wording.

## Motor Insurance
**Q: What is a No Claim Bonus (NCB)?**
A: NCB is a discount on your renewal premium if you have not made any claims in the preceding policy year.
