# Health Insurance Claim Process

## Cashless Claim

1. Visit a network hospital.
2. Submit your health card.
3. Hospital sends a pre-authorization request.
4. Digit verifies policy details.
5. Claim is approved or rejected.

## Reimbursement Claim

1. Customer pays hospital expenses.
2. Collect all invoices and reports.
3. Submit claim within 30 days.
4. Verification is completed.
5. Approved amount is transferred.
