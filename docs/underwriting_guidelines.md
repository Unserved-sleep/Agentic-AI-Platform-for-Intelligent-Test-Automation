# Underwriting Guidelines

## General Principles
1. Ensure full disclosure of material facts by the applicant.
2. Evaluate the risk based on age, occupation, and health status (for health) or vehicle age and type (for motor).

## Health Insurance Specific
- **Pre-existing Diseases (PED):** Must be declared. Standard waiting period of 2-4 years applies depending on the plan.
- **Age Limits:** Minimum entry age is 18 years for adults, 91 days for children. Maximum entry age is typically 65 years without special approvals.
- **Medical Tests:** Required for individuals above 45 years or for sum insured exceeding a specific threshold.

## Motor Insurance Specific
- **Vehicle Age:** Comprehensive coverage is generally restricted for vehicles older than 15 years unless inspected physically.
- **Modifications:** Any structural or performance modifications to the vehicle must be assessed and may incur an extra premium.
